Before You Buy

Questions people actually ask

Is this just another "digital nomad" listicle?

No. Each book is a field manual: cash-runway math, tax architecture, country-by-country visa dossiers, and negotiation scripts formatted to copy straight into WhatsApp or a lease. If a section doesn't change a decision you're about to make, it isn't in the book.

What's actually different between the three books?

Each book is a standalone 30+ page manual with its own operational framework tailored to that region — the tax rules, timezone math, and banking setup in the Nearshore Playbook are genuinely different from the Old World Playbook, not a reskin. All three share the same underlying 3-part structure: framework, dossiers, toolkit.

Do I need to buy all three?

No — each stands alone. Tier 2 and Tier 3 just happen to also include everything in the tier(s) below, so if you're already leaning toward Southeast Asia, Tier 3 gets you all 30 countries for one price instead of buying three separate books.

What do the Notion trackers and comic actually include?

The Notion Command Center is a set of editable boards for your runway budget, shortlist, and moving checklist. The comic (Leo Goes Remote) is an illustrated, ongoing story following one nomad's first month abroad — new pages ship as they're finished.

What format is it, and can I print it?

Print-ready PDFs, formatted for both screen reading and printing — the pre-departure checklists are designed to be printed and checked off by hand.

Is this legal or tax advice?

No. It's a research-backed operational framework, not a substitute for a licensed immigration attorney or tax professional — each book flags exactly when to bring one in.

Can I upgrade from Tier 1 to Tier 2 or 3 later?

Yes — each tier is priced to include everything below it, so upgrading only means paying the difference, not the full price again. Just reach out with your original order confirmation.
